# NPC Trainer Direction

## Core Tone

- Serious adventure with occasional humor.
- NPCs should feel like people in a dangerous world, not tutorial signs.
- Jokes should be light touches: dry comments, personality, or small awkward moments.

## Player Starting Situation

- The player is a survivor/exile getting a second chance.
- The starting area should feel like a place where the player must prove they can contribute.
- Trainers are not just handing out tools; they are testing whether the player can be trusted.

## Trainer Role

- Trainers are friendly mentors.
- Trainers also act like officials overseeing the player's second chance.
- They should be helpful, but they should still make the player earn trust through useful work.

## Early Progression Loop

The first connected skill loop should be:

```text
Woodcutting + Mining -> Smelting -> First gear
```

This keeps gathering and crafting connected from the beginning instead of teaching each skill in isolation.

## First Meaningful Goal

- Earn a second chance permit or badge.
- Help secure the settlement from a nearby threat.

The player should feel that early skilling is part of being allowed back into society and helping protect the starting settlement.

## Trainer Personalities

```text
Woodcutting Trainer: calm, outdoorsy, encouraging
Mining Trainer: gruff, practical, secretly kind
Crafting Trainer: clever, organized, a little sarcastic
Magic Trainer: mysterious, patient, slightly unsettling
```

## Dialogue Style

- Serious with occasional jokes.
- Trainers should explain what they want clearly, but with personality.
- Avoid overly modern jokes unless they are subtle.
- Avoid long lore dumps during early tutorial steps.

## Early Trainer Chain Direction

```text
1. Woodcutting Trainer introduces gathering wood.
2. Mining Trainer introduces gathering ore.
3. Smelting/crafting step turns gathered materials into first useful gear.
4. Trainers acknowledge the player is becoming useful.
5. Player works toward the first second-chance milestone.
```

## Design Reminder

Trainer dialogue should reinforce the progression backbone:

```text
combat leveling
gathering and crafting
quests and reputation
rare gear collection
boss progression
maxing long-term goals
```

The game is inspired by the long-term progression appeal of OSRS, but the trainer/progression structure should feel more connected and directed.
